# The owner's current quotation and invoice (reference for M3 Step 7–8 PDFs)

## The target: his letterhead quotation (2026-10-16, third file)

The owner then sent the quotation he makes in Word on his company letterhead, with: «ابغي تكون بهدليتر قريب هذا» and «بحسب اسم ومعلومات كل شركة» — BizCost's documents carry a **letterhead close to this one, built from each business's own name, logo and details**. It is the main reference; the two app documents below are the content to cover.

- **Letterhead on every page:**
  - header: the logo centred, under it the business name in English and in Arabic (two lines, bold); bold black diagonal bars in the top corners and a thin line across the top;
  - a large, light grey **watermark** of the logo behind the content;
  - footer: phone, email and location, each with an icon, inside a rounded bracket shape on the start side; bold diagonal bars in the bottom corner and a thin line across the bottom.
  - BizCost draws this from the business's logo, names (English + Arabic), contact details and one accent colour (his is black); no fixed artwork, so every business gets its own.
- **Opening:** "Dear <customer – area/city>," on the start side and "Date: 8 October 2026" on the end side, then a short sentence ("Thank you for your interest in our services. We are pleased to submit the following quotation for your review. All prices are in UAE Dirhams (AED).").
- **Lines table** with: No. | PIC (a photo of the item) | Area | Description | U.Price | QTY | TOTAL.
  - **PIC:** a picture per line (the line's attachment), cropped to fit.
  - **Area:** lines grouped by area or room (MAJLIS, Dining Room, Master BR, Room 1, Pantry); the area name written vertically in one merged cell spanning its lines.
  - **Description:** a bold item title, then "**Material:** …" (bold label) and fittings on their own lines; notes can stand out in red ("* Marble price to be added as per client selection + delivery").
  - **QTY with its unit** (m², RM, PCS); whole-number amounts when there are no fils.
  - The table header repeats on every page; each page keeps the letterhead.
- **Totals** under the table: SUB TOTAL (AED), VAT 5% (AED), TOTAL (AED).
- **Payment terms:** a table of stages (1st payment upon signing 50%, 2nd upon delivery 35%, 3rd after handover 15%) with each amount.
- **Validity:** "This quotation is valid for 7 days from the date of issue. Please feel free to contact us if you have any questions."
- **Signature:** the signatory's name, a signature and the company stamp (images).

## His two app documents (the content to cover)

Source: the owner, 2026-10-16, two PDFs his joinery company issues today from another invoicing app: an estimate (quotation) and an invoice with a payment. His brief: «ابغيه ارتب واجمل» — BizCost's documents must be **neater and more beautiful** than these. The files hold a real customer's name and phone, so they are not kept in the repository; this note records their structure only (placeholders, no customer data).

## What they hold (keep all of it)

- **Title** centred at the top: ESTIMATE / INVOICE.
- **Document box** (top right, a grey label column): number, date, due date (invoice), total.
- **Seller:** logo (invoice only), company name in large bold, "Reg. No" (it is the TRN), area and country, two phone numbers, email.
- **Buyer:** "Estimate To / Invoice To", the customer's name in bold, their phone.
- **Project or site** line above the table (e.g. the area the work is in), on the invoice.
- **Lines table:** #, item name in bold with a multi-line description under it (materials, finishes, fittings), quantity **with its unit** (SQM, RM, PCS), rate, discount (invoice), VAT, amount.
- **Totals** (bottom right): Sub Total, Discount, "VAT 5% on <taxable>", Total in a shaded row; on the invoice also Amount Paid and Amount Due.
- **QR code** at the foot (invoice), and a second page **Payment History** (payment no., date, amount, type, notes).

## What to do better

- **No empty half page:** the table and totals flow, totals follow the last line; a long table breaks across pages with its header repeated; the footer stays at the foot.
- **Bilingual and correct for the UAE:** Arabic + English labels on one page (Q23); "Tax Invoice / فاتورة ضريبية" for a VAT-registered seller (Q26), "TRN / الرقم الضريبي" instead of "Reg. No"; Arabic and Latin text in isolates (D-224).
- **One clear visual system:** the business's logo, a calm accent colour, consistent type sizes (IBM Plex Sans + Plex Sans Arabic, D-224), aligned numbers (tabular digits), light row rules instead of a grid of boxes.
- **Units always shown** with the quantity; descriptions keep their line breaks.
- **Discount before VAT** (D-220, D-221): today's estimate takes a 67.38 "discount" after VAT on the full subtotal to reach 16,100.00; BizCost's "Round the total" makes that adjustment before VAT, so the VAT line is right.
- **Payments on the same page** when they fit (a small "Payments" block under the totals), instead of a nearly empty second page.
- **A footer** with bank details and the default terms (Step 7, `businesses` document footer), the quotation's validity date, and room for a signature / stamp.
