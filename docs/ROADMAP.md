# BizCost Roadmap

Purpose: phases, milestone scope, step status, owner actions and deadline-bound open decisions. Other docs cover the rest: product scope (PRODUCT.md), technical design (ARCHITECTURE.md), tables (DATA_MODEL.md) and rationale (DECISIONS.md).

Last updated: 2026-10-04

Status legend: `DONE` · `IN PROGRESS` · `NEXT` · `PLANNED` · `BLOCKED (reason)` · `DEFERRED (by the owner)`

## How we work

- One milestone at a time. The owner approves each milestone's scope ("Proceed") before work starts.
- Do not build future phases early. Before their phase there is no cost engine, unit/inventory/waste calculation, AI, POS API, payroll, full VAT or advanced reporting. Do not create empty stub files for them either (e.g. no WAC file in M1).
- No placeholder screens. Each module manifest has `availability: 'released' | 'planned'`, and nav, tabs and "+" show only the released modules the business has enabled (ARCHITECTURE.md §Modules).
- Web leads. Mobile gets a flow only when its module ships.
- When a step completes: tick it here, update its status and "Last updated". Record new or changed decisions in DECISIONS.md, not here.
- Ask the owner only when a missing decision would cause significant rework.

Goal: every business records its sales the way it already sells, then sees its real profit (sales before VAT − materials at the frozen average − app fees − delivery it paid − the running-cost share by price, D-202 − the owner's time without a team) by product, order, day, month, channel and branch.

- [x] **Step 1: Domain groundwork for sales documents and real profit** (DONE 2026-10-04; decisions D-219–D-224). Pure code, and the Arabic PDF engine bake-off.
  - Built (pure TypeScript in `@bizcost/domain`, decimal.js only; it stores no data and releases nothing):
    - `computeSale`: VAT once per VAT rate, split back over the lines by largest remainder; prices before VAT or including VAT; zero-rated and exempt kept apart; nothing for a business not registered for VAT; refunds and credits as negative quantities; `pintLineOf` for IBR-147-AE (D-220). Purchases share its VAT helper (`documents/vat.ts`) with byte-identical results.
    - `solveRoundAmount`: "Round the total" before VAT, over the rates by their net, with a rounding difference of at most 0.02 and no line below zero (D-221).
    - `saleLineCost` and `fillSaleLineCost`: the cost of what was sold, frozen at the 90-day average as of the sale's day; "no price yet" filled once; the owner's time without a team (D-222).
    - Real profit: `saleLineProfit`, `sumRealProfit`, `businessRealProfit`, `rateSourceOf`, `rateCostsOf`, `saleRate`, `monthCostsSoFar`, `costsSpanOf`, `channelFeesOf`, `statementParts` (D-223).
    - Q5's consequences for D-109, D-114 rule 3, D-115, D-120, D-208 and P-001 (D-219).
  - Review fixes (2026-10-04, in D-220 and D-223):
    - a document discount on a sale whose taxed items mix prices with VAT and before VAT is refused (`document_discount_mixed_prices`), so a discount always takes exactly its amount off the net or off the total;
    - a rate shown while a month runs is never worked out from fewer than 7 days of sales: a first month with fewer runs on into the month after it;
    - the running month after a month without sales carries the last month that sold, never "awaiting sales" all month;
    - the business's total, which takes the month's costs off, is never flagged "before running costs".
  - PDF engine bake-off (D-224): Typst compiled to WebAssembly, run from Step 7 in a worker of the `/api/trpc` function that is replaced as its memory grows; headless Chromium is the fallback; react-pdf is rejected. Its text-layer limits (copy and search) are recorded for the tax agent's review.
  - Tests (514 in the package, 79 new):
    - the owner's examples as golden tests: 7 × 10.10 → VAT 3.54 (four lines of 0.51 and three of 0.50); "Round the total" 1,120.00 → 1,100.00 and mixed rates 2,600.00 → 2,500.00; 1,000.12 → 1,000.11 + 0.01; the Spanish Latte at 3.002034632035; the café's September (25%: the latte's 4.50; Talabat's 18% adds 3.24; the business 34,200); 20,000 ÷ 30,000; a first month's 6,000 × 15 ÷ 30 = 3,000 ÷ 12,000 = 25%; the home baker's order #1001 at 35.10;
    - fast-check: VAT per rate = net × rate (S-09) and the line VAT adds up to it; a typed total is reached within the rounding difference; a document discount means one thing; a finished month's shares add up to its costs and a channel's fees to its statements; a rate shown while its month runs comes from at least 7 days of sales; a refund mirrors its sale; a cost once set never changes; a business not registered for VAT never gets a VAT amount; every line maps (IBR-147-AE); purchases byte-identical to the engine before the move;
    - the review's adversary cases (`sale.vat-adversary`, `real-profit.adversary`).
