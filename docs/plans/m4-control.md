## Milestone 4 = Phase 4 Control

Goal: a business that keeps stock knows **how much it has** in each branch and **where money is lost**: usage beyond what its sales (and production) needed, waste, and what each costs per week, per month and per year, said in neutral words (PRODUCT.md §4 rule 5: never "theft").

- **The words** (PRODUCT.md §13), for one material in one branch between two counts:
  - **Actual usage** = the stock at the earlier count + what came in (purchases, transfers in, production) − what went back (returns, transfers out) − the stock at the later count.
  - **Expected usage** = what sales took by their recipes (frozen in `sale_line_materials`, D-219) + what production took (Q14) + what was taken out for work (Q13).
  - **Waste** = usage someone explained: recorded as it happened (Q7) or given a reason at the count (Q6).
  - **Unexplained usage** = actual − expected − waste. It can be below zero ("less than expected").
- **Stock starts with a count.** Until a material's first count nothing about it changes (D-219): sales freeze its cost at the 90-day average and take nothing out. The first count starts its stock; from then on sales, waste and work take it out, and each later count shows what didn't match.
- **The money side.** Waste and unexplained usage are part of True Cost (PRODUCT.md §4 rule 7), so the business's real profit takes them off (Q9). Profit by product stays at its recipe: what it earns when made as planned (rule 6's "theoretical"), shown beside what was lost.
- **Release A, half-way through:** Stock (counts, stock on hand, sales and work taking stock out, moving stock between branches).
- **Release B, last:** Usage & Waste (recording waste, reasons, the Monitor, alerts), with production and prepared items (Q14).

Estimate: **~16 weeks solo** on the M1–M3 basis (M2 9–11, M3 20–23). M4 is about 1.5× M2: it adds three document kinds (counts, waste and work, transfers; production with Q14), the stock side of every sale posting, and the Monitor.

- **Per step, in weeks:** 1 (1.5), 2 (2.5), 3 (2.5), 4 (1.5), 5 (0.5), 6 (2.5), 7 (1), 8 (2), 9 (0.5), 10 (1.5).
- **Real pace so far:** M2 built in 5 working days (about 8 with the owner's rounds); M3 ran 2026-10-04 → 10-21 for 20–23 estimated weeks.
- **At that pace,** M4 builds in roughly **9–11 working days**: about **2–3 weeks of calendar time** with the owner's answers and his two reviews.
- **What can slow it:** the 17 answers (Q14 alone moves Step 8); the sales posting change under concurrency (Step 3); the import's time budget once sales take stock out.

Release rule (as M3):

- **Before their release step,** `inventory` and `usage_waste` stay `planned`: their screens run only in tests and on local development servers with the dev-only preview (`BIZCOST_PREVIEW_MODULES`, D-125); their e2e specs run on `next dev` (D-127).
- **Release A (Step 5):** `inventory`. **Release B (Step 9):** `usage_waste`. Each keeps the "soon" chip in Customize BizCost and Smart Setup's review until then (D-003, D-075). After each release, every spec of the released module runs on the production build (D-199).
- **Smart Setup already writes `business_modules` rows** for both where `keeps_stock` is on (PRODUCT.md §6.6, §6.10 invariant 5), so each release shows them at once there. A business without `keeps_stock` sees no change at all.
- **Each module gets its keys, template defaults and backfill migration** in the step that builds it (D-124).
- **Released businesses see no change before Release A.** No material is tracked until someone counts it, so even after Release A a business that never counts keeps M3's behaviour exactly.
- Web only: mobile and the hosted deploy stay DEFERRED by the owner. Counting is a phone task: the web works on phones (as every M2–M3 screen); the native app stays deferred.

## Steps

- [x] **Step 1: Domain groundwork for stock and usage** (DONE 2026-10-31; D-340–D-342; status in ROADMAP.md). Pure TypeScript in `@bizcost/domain`, decimal.js only, as M2 Step 1 and M3 Step 1. It stores no data and releases nothing.
  - **Counts** (`stock/count.ts`): a counted line typed in any unit or pack of its material, or several ("3 cartons + 4 bottles"), to one base quantity through the units engine (D-108); the difference against the book quantity; splitting a difference by reasons, never past the difference, signs kept.
  - **Usage between two counts** (`stock/usage.ts`), per branch and material: actual, expected, waste, unexplained, their values, "% more / less than expected" (actual vs expected), within the tolerance or not (Q10). Impact from the period's daily rate: per week (÷ days × 7) and a yearly estimate (÷ days × 365); by month, each count's amounts count on its day (Q9).
  - **Late entries** (`stock/late.ts`, Q5): which count absorbs an entry dated on or before it (the first finalized count of its branch and material on or after its day), and the offset that keeps that count's stock.
  - **The average** (`costing/wac.ts`, D-109 extended): `wacOpen` (a first count starts the cost row at the counted quantity × that day's 90-day average, Q3); count differences in and out at the average; a refund back to stock at the cost it went out (Q8); production (components out, the made goods in at exactly their value, Q14); transfers change no cost (D-114 rule 5). Negative stock per Q4 (`shortfall`, then the next receipt's `adjustment`, as built).
  - **Recipes** (Q14, Q15): a line's preparation loss (200 g with 20% lost counts 250 g, in expected usage and in the cost); a prepared item used in recipes, its cost from its own recipe (on read) or its stock average once tracked, with a cycle check and a depth limit.
  - **Low stock and below zero** (Q10): the level check per branch and material.
  - **Tests:** goldens from the definition of done below; fast-check properties: conservation (value = Σ in − Σ out − Σ adjustments) with counts and transfers; the usage identity; late entries in any order; reasons never past the difference; production conserves value.
- [ ] **Step 2: The first count, counts and stock on hand** (data, API and web; `inventory` stays `planned`; needs Q2, Q3, Q6, Q16).
  - **Data:** `stock_counts` (`location_id`, `business_date` = the stock at the end of that day, `kind` `opening` | `count`, `status` `draft` | `final` | `discarded`, reversal columns as purchases) and `stock_count_lines` (`material_id`, the parts as typed, `counted_base_qty`, at finalize `book_base_qty`, the difference and its value; who counted and when, per line); `materials.stock_tracked_from` (Q2 = A; replaces D-115's `businesses.first_stock_count_at`); movement kinds `opening` and `count` with their CHECK shapes and unique indexes. RLS, touch, audit, pgTAP.
  - **The first count** ("Start tracking your stock" / «ابدأ متابعة مخزونك»): pick the materials (all, or a few to start, Q2), count each in every branch the same day; finalizing starts their stock (Q3). It shows no unexplained usage: there is nothing to compare yet.
  - **Later counts:** of a branch, of all its tracked materials or a few; saved line by line, so several people can count one sheet (Q6); blind for whoever only counts; the review (expected vs counted, the difference in quantity and, with the costs switch, in AED; "Count again" on a line); finalize posts the differences and sets the stock. A finalized count is reversed only while it is the latest count of each of its lines (an opening count only while nothing has taken stock out since).
  - **API:** `stock.list` (on hand per branch, in the material's biggest pack and its counting unit; value only with the costs switch), `stock.card` (a material's movements in a branch, by day), `count.*` (start, save a line, get (blind for counters), list, finalize, discard, reverse), `stock.startTracking`. D-115's read switches per material: a tracked material reads its stock average.
  - **Web** (`features/stock`): the Stock page (tracked materials, on hand per branch said in words, "12 cartons + 3 bottles (147 L)", value locked without the costs switch, last counted; the empty state that starts tracking); the count sheet, phone first (search, "not counted yet", − / +, packs); the review; the stock card. Business wording: "Ingredients & supplies", "Goods" (D-117, D-118).
  - **Tests:** pgTAP, API (counts, blind reads, reversal rules, the cost the day before and after a first count), web Vitest, `stock.spec.ts` (English desktop, Arabic phone) on the preview server.
- [ ] **Step 3: Sales and work take stock out** (needs Q4, Q5, Q8, Q9, Q13).
  - **Posting:** once a material is tracked, every finalized sale that uses it (Today's sales, One sale, an import, an order's delivery, an issued invoice's sale) issues it from the sale's branch: one movement per sale and material (`sale`), its cost frozen in `sale_line_materials` with a new basis `stock`. Reversing the sale reverses its movements (replay, D-135). Untracked materials keep D-219 exactly.
  - **Refunds by quantity** (Q8): back to stock, or recorded as waste "returned by a customer".
  - **Below zero** (Q4); **late entries** (Q5): the offset movement `count_late`, and the count shows "changed after the count by N entries".

- [ ] **Step 6: Usage & Waste: recording waste, reasons and the Monitor** (needs Q7, Q9, Q10).
  - **Record waste** (Q7) / «سجّل هدرًا»: a material or a product (a product's waste is its recipe's materials: a failed print, a dropped cake), the quantity in any unit or pack, the reason (waste or spillage, damaged or expired, staff use, free or complimentary, failed piece or offcut, returned by a customer, other), the branch, the day, a note and a photo. ## Technical choices (no owner answer needed)

Recorded as decisions when built.

- **Per-material tracking** (if Q2 = A): `materials.stock_tracked_from`, set by the material's opening count; D-115's switch moves from the business to the material.
- **Movement kinds** added to `stock_movements`: `opening`, `count`, `count_late`, `sale`, `sale_return`, `usage` (waste or work), `transfer_out`, `transfer_in`, and with Q14 `production_out`, `production_in`; `reversal` covers each. One CHECK per kind's shape; unique partial indexes so each source line posts once.
- **A sale issues once per material**, not per line: about 600 movements for a branch's month of imported summaries; its lines share the issue's unit cost in `sale_line_materials` (basis `stock`).
- **Lock order unchanged** (D-110, D-135); a count, a waste record, a transfer and a production are documents locked first.
- **Read models:** stock on hand from `stock_balances`; usage between counts on read from the ledger (an index on `(business_id, location_id, material_id, business_date)`); alerts on read within the Dashboard's budget. No stored snapshots (D-186).
- **Counts saved line by line** with a version per line, so two counters never overwrite each other.
